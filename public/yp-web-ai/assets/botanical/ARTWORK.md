# Botanical Atelier artwork

Saved project assets: [wide artwork](skincare-wide.png), [portrait artwork](skincare-portrait.png). Booth previews: [front-left](../peninsular-templates/penin-botanical-atelier.png), [front-right](../peninsular-templates/penin-botanical-atelier-alt.png).

Generated using the built-in image generation tool (imagegen skill). Original PNGs are preserved unchanged; the actual Yuppie vector logo is added separately by template-branding.js. No reference brand names, advertising claims, seals or watermarks are reproduced. References describe the same booth from two angles. Dimensions are visual estimates, not fabrication drawings.

## Wide prompt
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: horizontal photographic skincare advertisement background texture for a 3D exhibition booth lightbox, flat artwork only. Create a photorealistic premium natural skincare campaign: warm ivory background, an adult woman with long chestnut hair and natural skin holding a small plain white cosmetic serum bottle on the right half, fresh lime-green botanical leaves at upper right, a few clean white skincare bottles with pale peach caps at lower right. Left 45 percent is gently lit ivory negative space for an actual logo to be added separately. Landscape ratio about 2.4:1. Soft luminous studio beauty lighting, natural pores, cream and lime-green palette. Absolutely no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no certification seals, no border, no booth or mockup.

## Portrait prompt
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: flat portrait skincare advertisement artwork for a vertical exhibition booth display. Photorealistic close-up portrait of an adult woman with chestnut hair and natural skin, face occupying lower two thirds, with a fresh green leaf beside her cheek and plain white serum bottle at bottom. Warm ivory and lime green botanical background. Upper quarter quiet ivory negative space for a real logo added later. Premium soft studio beauty lighting, realistic skin detail. Portrait 3:4. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no seals, no borders, no mockup.
